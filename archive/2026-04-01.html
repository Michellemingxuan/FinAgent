## NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) — Financial Analysis NVIDIA has delivered extraordinary revenue growth, with the top line expanding from $60.9B in FY2024 to $215.9B in FY2026 — a 3.5x increase in just two years — driven by insatiable demand for AI/data center compute, while operating margins expanded from 54.1% to 60.4%, reflecting powerful operating leverage despite massive investment in R&D and supply chain capacity.
