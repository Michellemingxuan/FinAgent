Supply Chain Map
Micron Technology occupies a critical mid-tier position in the semiconductor supply chain as one of only three major global producers of DRAM and NAND flash memory, sitting downstream of specialized capital equipment makers and upstream of nearly every major computing, mobile, cloud, and automotive platform. Its highly capital-intensive, vertically integrated manufacturing model creates deep dependencies on a small number of equipment suppliers while serving a broad but concentrated customer base where AI-driven HBM demand from companies like NVIDIA is rapidly reshaping revenue mix.
