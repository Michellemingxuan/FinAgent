Supply Chain Map
IREN Limited sits at the intersection of energy-intensive Bitcoin mining and high-performance AI data center infrastructure, making it critically dependent on upstream GPU and ASIC hardware suppliers (primarily NVIDIA and Bitmain) as well as low-cost electricity providers for its Canadian and Australian operations. On the downstream side, IREN monetizes its operations primarily through Bitcoin market sales via exchanges like Coinbase, while increasingly targeting enterprise AI/HPC customers to lease GPU compute capacity, diversifying revenue beyond pure crypto mining.
